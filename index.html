📫 Send your items, we'll handle the rest!
We offer our services and time to get your favorite stuff signed by your favorite celebrities! Based in New Jersey, we attend as many cons along the east coast as we can — from D.C. to Boston. Interested in a con we're attending? Reach out!

Pricing Tiers
Tier 1
Flattened standard 4" Funkos, prints sized 8x10–11x17, trading cards, and other small flat items.
Tier 2
Unflattened (in box) 4" Funkos, 3D figures, small posters (under 24"x36"), and other medium flat items.
Tier 3
Funkos larger than standard 4" size, 24"x36" posters, and other larger flat items.
Tier 4
10", Moment, Deluxe, Comic Cover, etc. Funkos, posters 40"+, helmets, swords, and other oversized items.
➕ New Funko windows available for $5 (no swaps, but we can point you to people who do)
🛡 Authentication services priced at show rates, typically $10–15 depending on the company
🎨 Quotes & character names are up to guest discretion — 2 colors max, 1 quote max. Drawings/Kanji not encouraged: you can include it on your post-it but we will not request or point it out to guests
💰 Any upcharges must be paid upon request after the event — items will not ship until paid
⚓ Captain's Orders! (What we need from you)
⚠ Everything must arrive by the DUE date for each con — often within a week of the event!
  1. 📦 The item you want signed (Funkos, comics, action figures, etc.)
  2. 📝 Post-it on the backside: your name & address
  3. 📝 Post-it on the frontside: what you'd like on the item (colors, quote, character name, personalization, etc.)
  4. 💲 Price of the autograph + authentication fee
  5. 🤝 Consignment fee
  6. 🚚 Return shipping payment or a return label (you buy it and send it to us)
  7. 🛡 Highly encouraged: a protector for your item (Pop Shield Armor, top loader, etc.)
PayPal (F&F preferred; G&S you cover the fee) Zelle
  • All we can guarantee is the AUTOGRAPH — we cannot guarantee exact placement, character names, quotes, colors, drawings, etc.
  • If you cancel after sending your item, you must cover the consignment fee and return shipping.
  • Delayed/late packages that miss the event: all funds and items returned after the event, but you pay return shipping. Same applies to guest cancellations (full refund, you cover return shipping).
  • All funds must be sent by the due date, otherwise a 5% fee increase applies to cover instant transfer.
  • Pre-purchased tickets with an "Admin Fee" or "Convenience Fee" are paid by you.
  • Payment for shipping is expected within a week of the event. We are not a storage unit and will not hold your item.
⚓ By sending your items and funds, you accept all these terms.
